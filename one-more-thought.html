The Game

You are in bed. Nothing is happening around you. The problem is everything happening inside your head.

One More Thought is a psychological night management game. You manage your own mental and physical state as the hours tick toward morning — through 60 dynamic events across three phases of the night.

Small decisions add up fast. Every choice shifts your stats. The night has rules you'll only learn by losing.
