Frequently Asked

How can I purchase your books?

Our books are available on multiple platforms including Amazon, Apple Books, Kobo, and others. Click the "Get It Now" button on any book to see all available stores.

When will your games be released?

Our games are currently in development. Mountain Tales is scheduled for early 2025 and Heartfall for mid 2025. Follow us on social media for updates!

Can I collaborate with Grass Studio?

Yes! We're open to collaborations. Please send your proposal to grass.stu@gmail.com with the subject "Collaboration Inquiry".

Do you accept submissions for game/book ideas?

While we appreciate creative ideas, we're currently not accepting unsolicited submissions due to our full development schedule.
