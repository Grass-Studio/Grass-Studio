The Game

About Heartfall

Heartfall is an open world exploration game where players travel between mysterious dimensions searching for rare treasures and ancient secrets.

The world is huge and filled with mountains, forests, rivers and strange places hidden deep underground. Most creatures in the main world are peaceful, but other dimensions are far more dangerous.

World

Dimensions

The Overworld

The default world where the adventure begins. It is the largest dimension in the game with mountains reaching up to 2000 units high, large plains, rivers, forests and swamps. Players spend most of their journey here. The inhabitants of this world are mostly peaceful.

The Underworld

This world lies deep underground. The entrance is a massive cave that acts as a giant mining tunnel beneath the cliffs of the overworld. The area is covered in heavy fog and toxic gas. Time itself feels strange here. The only biome is the Dead Soil biome, a cold land filled with deadly gas pits.

Sky Dimension

A smaller floating dimension above the world. The entrance appears on a remote island and opens only when a special key is used. This world is full of gemstones and beautiful clouds. There are two land types: peaceful white clouds and dangerous dark clouds.
