About Us

This studio didn't start with a big plan. It started with learning, curiosity, and small random games.

During the challenging times of COVID-19, Grass Studio emerged as an experimental passion project. What began as a simple curiosity evolved into a creative powerhouse dedicated to crafting immersive experiences.

Today, we continue our journey of learning and building, pushing the boundaries of what's possible in gaming and storytelling.
