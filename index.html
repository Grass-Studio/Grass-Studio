Our Story

Born from
Curiosity

This studio didn't start with a grand plan. It started with learning, late nights, and small random games built for the love of it.

During the uncertainty of COVID-19, Grass Studio emerged as a passion experiment. Two young creators in Algeria, building worlds from scratch — no budget, no blueprint, just relentless curiosity.

Today we make indie games and horror fiction that we'd want to experience ourselves. No genre police. No limits.
