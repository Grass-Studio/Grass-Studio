The Game

An open world exploration game where players travel between mysterious dimensions searching for rare treasures and ancient secrets.

The world is massive — mountains, forests, rivers and strange places hidden deep underground. Most creatures in the main world are peaceful, but cross into the other dimensions and the danger multiplies fast.

Every decision shapes your path. Every dimension has its own rules. Your journey begins in a peaceful land... but not everything is what it seems.

The Three
Dimensions

Dimension I · Default World

The Overworld

The largest dimension — where the adventure begins. Mountains reach 2,000 units high, vast plains stretch between rivers and forests. Inhabitants are mostly peaceful. This is the world you'll know best, and still never fully map.

Dimension II · Deep Below

The Underworld

Accessed through a massive cave beneath the overworld cliffs. Heavy fog. Toxic gas. Time itself feels wrong here. The only biome: Dead Soil — a cold, silent land filled with gas pits and creatures that have never seen the sun.

Dimension III · High Above

Sky Dimension

A floating world accessible only by special key, found on a remote island. Gemstones embedded in clouds. Two land types: peaceful white clouds and treacherous dark ones. Beautiful. Lethal. Worth every risk.

Bestiary

World
Creatures

Underworld · Hostile

Mobles

Slow stone creatures formed by ancient underground gases. They move in groups, attack by throwing rocks, and generate toxic fog that kills quickly. Rarely threatened — but when they are, they become something terrifying.

Sky Dimension · Guardian

Flogst Core

An ancient guardian with a core heart and four protective pillars. Once used in battles long forgotten, it now defends the peaceful sky lands. It leaps high, moves fast, and carries a hidden wind shield that repels anything airborne.

Sky Dimension · Rare

Moblz

A rare sky creature seen hovering near dark cloud formations. Little is known about its origins — only that encountering one means you've gone somewhere most players never reach.

Overworld · Mysterious

Unknown

The overworld holds creatures yet to be fully catalogued. Some say certain mountains move at night. Others claim the forests have a rhythm. Explore and see what you find.
