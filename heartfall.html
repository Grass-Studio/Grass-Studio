Heartfall

An open-world fantasy exploration game where freedom comes first. Wander through a petrified world, discover forgotten places, and uncover the strange creatures that inhabit it.

About the Game

Heartfall focuses on exploration rather than strict story progression. Players can wander the world freely, finding hidden ruins, mysterious creatures, and fragments of a forgotten civilization. Every location hints at a past that slowly reveals itself.

Screenshots

World Regions

Mystic Forest

A quiet forest filled with old ruins and ancient magic. Hidden paths lead explorers deeper into forgotten places.

Stone Plains

A vast petrified landscape where remnants of lost civilizations stand frozen in time.

Crystal Caverns

Underground caves glowing with strange crystals that seem to hold powerful magical energy.

Creatures

Shadow Wolf

A fast predator that hunts at night and stalks travelers across the darker parts of the world.

Stone Guardian

Ancient statues that awaken when ruins are disturbed, protecting the secrets hidden within.

Crystal Spirit

A rare magical entity found near crystal caverns, appearing only to those who wander far enough.
